**Role in launch:** the series workhorse — the promise in one card. TOFU→BOFU. **No face required** — screen recording + push-ins.

## Final script / voiceover text (~22s)

> This checking account has $12,450 just… sitting. Not wrong — just idle. Every month it sits, it earns basically nothing, quietly. Here's the same account in VAULT. It flags the idle cash as a move — with the number, what it could be earning in a high-yield account instead, and how long the move takes. Under a minute. That's the whole idea: not charts. Moves.

*(Numbers spoken must match the demo account's on-screen card — currently $12,450 / +$560/yr from the seeded review fixture. If the fixture changes, re-read the line with the on-screen figure.)*
